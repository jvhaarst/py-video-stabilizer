Video Stabilizer

Drop in a video. It measures the camera path, asks you to mark the horizon on a few frames, then re-renders the clip with the roll locked and the canvas expanded so nothing is cropped. Everything runs in this page — no upload, no network, no dependencies.

Drop a video here or click to choose one — it never leaves your machine

Working

Drag an endpoint to rotate, the middle to slide. A accept, S skip, R reset, ,. fine rotate. Snapping is manual on purpose: it chases the strongest edge nearby, which is often a field boundary rather than the horizon.
